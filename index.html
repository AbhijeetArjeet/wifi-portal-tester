📱 How to Install as PWA App for Offline Use ▼

Android / Chrome:

  1. Tap the browser menu (3 dots at top right).
  2. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".

iPhone / iOS Safari:

  1. Tap the Share button (box with arrow pointing up) at bottom bar.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".

Once installed, tap the icon directly on your phone's home screen even when completely offline!

📖 Safe Auto-Relogin & Anti-Ban System ▼

Anti-Ban Safeguard:

To prevent captive portal gateways from banning your account or MAC address due to request spamming, the PWA enforces a mandatory 30-second cooldown delay between login attempts.

Recommended Safe Timer:

Set the background timer interval to 30 or 60 minutes for optimal connection stability without triggering server limits.
